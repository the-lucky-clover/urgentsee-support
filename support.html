A little help, when it matters

Let’s get you connected.

Start with these common fixes. For anything else, contact the developer directly.

Email support

Support, privacy requests, and abuse reports: theluckyclover@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model, and a short description. Never send passwords, session tokens, or private message content unless you choose to share it for the report.

Common questions

How do I pair two devices?

Both people need UrgentSee. In Recipients, choose My QR on one device. On the other, choose Scan / enter and scan the QR or enter the short code. Codes expire after 15 minutes; create a new one if needed. Only share codes with people you want in your circle.

Why didn’t a notification arrive or make a sound?

Open Settings → Notifications in UrgentSee and check your system notification settings. Allow alerts and sounds, confirm an internet connection, and review Focus and silent mode. Keep both apps up to date. A successful send is not a guarantee that a notification was displayed or read.

How do I hide message text on the lock screen?

In UrgentSee, open Settings → Privacy & data and turn on Hide text in sent notifications. New sends use a private notice. This does not change previews already delivered to another device.

Why can’t I send an attachment by itself?

Add a typed or quick message with your attachment, then dismiss the keyboard to reveal Slide to send. You can attach up to four files with a combined size of 25 MB.

How do I block someone or report a concern?

Open Recipients, use that person’s actions menu, and choose Block to stop further messages through the pairing. For harassment, objectionable content, or other concerns, email the support contact. Describe what happened and share only the information you want reviewed. Reports are reviewed by the developer; there is no automated monitoring of decrypted message bodies.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Privacy & data → Delete account and data. Review the confirmation and choose Delete account. Your device account closes and local history is removed; encrypted uploads are queued for background deletion. Pairings are removed. Copies already saved by recipients may remain. See the privacy policy for retained records. If you cannot access the app, email the support contact for help verifying and handling your request.

How do I request a refund?

App Store purchases and refunds are managed by Apple. Visit Apple’s Report a Problem page and follow the options available for your purchase.
