UrgentSee gives important messages a dedicated place. Pair with someone you trust, write what matters, and slide to send.

Get help with UrgentSeeUnderstand your privacy

Start with your circle.

  1. Open Recipients and show your pairing QR or code.
  2. Have the other person scan or enter it in UrgentSee.
  3. Select them in Dispatch, enter a message, and slide to send.

Make it yours.

Add context with photos, video, files, and links. Choose a notification tone, adjust text size, or reduce motion.

Message bodies and attachments are encrypted. You choose whether new notification previews show your message text.

How notification previews work →

Both people need UrgentSee and an internet connection. Permissions, Focus, silent mode, and connectivity affect notifications. UrgentSee is not an emergency-response service.
