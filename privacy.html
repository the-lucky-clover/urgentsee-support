Privacy policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

UrgentSee is developed and operated by Steven Pounds. This policy explains how the app, messaging service, and this website handle information. For privacy requests, contact theluckyclover@gmail.com.

Information used to run UrgentSee

  • Device accounts: an installation identifier, public encryption key, device display name, authentication record, and notification token allow us to connect your device and deliver messages.
  • Pairings and delivery: the service stores relationships between paired accounts, block status, sender and recipient identifiers, message identifiers, timestamps, delivery/read status, retry information, and operational diagnostics.
  • Messages and uploads: full message bodies and attachments are encrypted on the sending device for the recipient. The service stores encrypted content and ownership metadata to deliver it. Notification previews are handled separately, as explained below.
  • Support: if you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. Avoid sending sensitive content that is not needed to resolve your request.

Readable notification previews

Readable previews are enabled by default. These previews may contain message text in plaintext and are processed by our messaging service and Apple’s or Google’s notification service. They may appear on the recipient’s lock screen.

Turn on Hide text in sent notifications in Settings → Privacy & data to replace message text with a private notice for new sends. This setting does not remove previews that have already been delivered.

Information kept on your device

Private encryption keys, locally assigned recipient names, selected contact details, profile photos, quick-message templates, preferences, and encrypted message archives are stored on your device. UrgentSee does not upload your address book. Contacts, camera, microphone, and selected photo/file access are used only for the features you choose to use. System permissions can be changed in your device settings.

Service providers and sharing

Cloudflare provides the backend infrastructure used for account records, encrypted uploads, delivery coordination, and operational logs. Apple Push Notification service and, for Android recipients, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver notifications. Providers may process network identifiers, request metadata, and diagnostics to operate and protect their services.

Messages and selected attachments are shared with the recipients you choose. We do not include advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs in UrgentSee. We do not sell personal information. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to investigate abuse and protect users or the service.

Retention

Active account records and pairings are retained while needed to provide the service. Message status, readable previews, and encrypted uploads may remain after a notification’s retry window ends; that window is not a content-deletion timer. Uploaded attachments do not currently have a general automatic expiry.

Operational logs, security/rate-limit records, support correspondence, and infrastructure backups may remain separately for service operation, troubleshooting, abuse prevention, recovery, and legal obligations. Retention depends on the record’s purpose and the provider configuration. Contact us for information about a specific request.

Account deletion and your choices

Use Settings → Privacy & data → Delete account and data to close this device’s account. After confirmation by the service, current account and pairing records, associated server message records, and that account’s telemetry are removed. The app clears its session, keys, local recipient profiles, templates, and message history.

Encrypted uploads involving the account and its notification tokens/preferences are deleted by a resumable background job. The job runs immediately and is retried by a five-minute schedule; large queues or service failures can delay completion. A hashed revocation record remains for up to 367 days so previously issued sessions cannot be reused. Outstanding short pairing codes expire after 15 minutes.

Deletion cannot remove copies already saved by recipients, and separate operational logs or backups may remain as described above. You can connect again later as a new account. To request access, correction, deletion assistance, or information about applicable privacy rights, email our privacy contact. We may need to verify your connection to the account.

This website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use advertising, analytics scripts, or cookies set by this site. GitHub may process request information, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement. Email links open your chosen email application.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The current policy and its update date will be available here. Contact theluckyclover@gmail.com with questions.
